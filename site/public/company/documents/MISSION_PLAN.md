# XPeX Company Preparation Mission — V1

Prepared 10 October 2026 under the owner's authorization to standardize and package the company for announcement, investment review or possible acquisition.

## Scope delivered in this release

1. Common company preparation structure for all seven public system profiles.
2. Public company preparation center and seven dossiers with canonical stages preserved.
3. System packs, AI-BOM records, documented trust boundaries and transfer checklists.
4. Guided company demonstration with dated public HTTP observations and a bounded Factory workflow verification.
5. Proposed diagnostic/pilot, contribution-margin scenario calculator and acceptance criteria.
6. Public review package and private document request index.
7. Calendar-date rendering correction and recorded accelerator eligibility review.

This release standardizes the company-level presentation and documentation. It does not reimplement or rebrand every separate product runtime. Other product repositories and private/customer workflows require their own scoped engineering changes and review.

## Announcement criteria

Announce the actual company offering and the capabilities demonstrated with current evidence. Clearly separate a proposal, available interface, tested workflow and accepted customer result. A complete commercial launch requires an owner-approved offer and contact process. Investor acceptance, paid revenue and transaction readiness remain separate claims.

## Sale preparation criteria

Before a transaction commitment: establish title and transfer scope, reconcile financial obligations, verify operational handover, document risks, review valuation assumptions and obtain qualified legal/accounting review. The owner's approval is required for binding terms, account transfer, signature or asset movement.

The nine current company gates are machine-readable at `data/company/company-readiness-v1.json`. Written preparation is not independent acceptance. No outreach, application submission, binding contract, purchase or transfer follows automatically from this release.
