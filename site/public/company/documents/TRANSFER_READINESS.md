# XPeX — Technical and Asset Transfer Preparation

Status: DUE_DILIGENCE_REQUIRED. No claim that every asset is legally transferable or that a sale has been approved.

## Asset register

The seven system preparation packs contain source references, default-branch observations, runtime metadata, evidence links, limits, costs marked unverified and a transfer checklist. A repository under the corporate GitHub identity proves a source location, not a complete chain of legal title.

The source metadata observation is `data/company/preparation-source-observations-v1.json`. GitHub reports Academy's repository license metadata as AGPL-3.0. Factory's metadata is NOASSERTION; other observed repositories return no SPDX license metadata. These values are observations, not a completed license audit. Review actual license files, upstream code, contributions and dependency obligations before negotiating rights. Commercial use and selling software copies can be permitted under free-software licenses, while their obligations remain. This does not establish exclusive ownership or resolve the rights in a company transaction. GNU reference: https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/selling.en.html

## Required review by asset

- Legal owner and documents supporting title, including contractors/contributors and upstream/fork lineage.
- Rights actually offered: code, brand, domains, documentation, customer contracts and data rights.
- Dependency licenses, redistribution/source obligations and third-party content.
- Provider terms for transferring a project, organization, billing and administration.
- Personal-account dependencies and replacement of credentials by the new authorized operator.
- Contract assignment requirements, outstanding charges and obligations.
- Installation, backup, restore, rollback, monitoring and second-operator execution evidence.
- Post-transfer support scope, acceptance and revocation of old access.

Credentials, seeds, private keys, personal ChatGPT sessions and personal connector connections are not part of the public review package. New owners must establish their own authorized access. Personal account connections do not prove a transferable backend integration.

## Handover rehearsal

Use an isolated authorized environment. A second operator must deploy from a pinned commit, configure their own access, run the agreed smoke tests, restore a test backup and demonstrate rollback. Record commands with sensitive values removed, timestamps, results and limitations. Do not destroy current environments during the rehearsal.

## Financial and legal scope

Distinguish an investment, purchase of company control and purchase of specified assets. Identify assets and obligations before proposing a price. Review valuation assumptions, contracts, tax implications and title with qualified legal/accounting advisers before agreeing terms.

Preparation references:

- SBA, general preparation/valuation and asset/obligation inventory: https://legacy.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/close-or-sell-your-business (US guidance; not a substitute for the applicable local transaction rules).

No numeric valuation or transaction commitment is included in this preparation.
