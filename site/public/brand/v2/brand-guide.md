## Visual rules

Near-black `#07090D`, warm flat gold `#E2B248`, white `#FFFFFF` and graphite define the company palette. Gold signals identity and hierarchy. Status indicators remain separate and require evidence. A logo does not confer assurance, admission or financial readiness.

Use gold on dark surfaces, white when color is unavailable and ink on light surfaces. Keep clear space of at least 25% of the symbol width. The minimum recommended symbol size is 24 CSS pixels. Preserve geometry, aspect ratio and naming. Avoid bevels, gradients, shadows on marks and decorative backgrounds inside symbols.

The symbols are original geometric drawings. Reference to contemporary technology design concerns simplicity and consistency; it does not imply affiliation with other companies or legal registration of these marks.

## Production sources and exports

- Source geometry and website components: `site/src/brand.js`.
- Regenerate the public SVG files: `node scripts/build-brand-assets.mjs`.
- Public assets: `site/public/brand/v2/`.
- Manifest: `site/public/brand/v2/manifest.json`.
- Public identity page: `https://xpex-systems-ai.vercel.app/?view=brand`.

Each identity has gold, white and ink SVG symbols plus a horizontal gold lockup. The symbol paths are vector geometry. Lockup text uses Inter with Arial/sans-serif fallback; supply or outline a licensed font for print workflows where exact typography is required.

The downloadable kit includes the source SVGs, the identity sheet, manifest and this guide. The social preview PNG is a rendering of source vector geometry. Image-generated brand presentation artwork is supporting material; the SVGs remain the authoritative production marks.

## Voice and commercial truth

Master tagline: **Build. Connect. Operate. Prove.**

GXEON economy line: **Build. Connect. Earn. Verify.**

Descriptor: **AI Systems, Agents & Company Intelligence**.

Write confidently, minimally and with evidence. Avoid invented customers, partnerships, revenue, certification and unsupported superlatives. The investor narrative must preserve pre-seed commercial maturity, AL1 assurance and each system's actual admission/runtime gates.
