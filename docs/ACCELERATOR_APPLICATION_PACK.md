**XPeX Systems AI composes existing software, MCP tools, plugins, skills and governed AI agents into evidence-backed digital workers that expand human capability.**

## What problem are we solving?

Companies already have email, cloud, code, databases, CRMs, design tools and AI models — but these systems remain fragmented.

The new generation of AI agents can reason across tools, but enterprises still need a safe way to answer:

- what can the agent access?
- which system is canonical?
- what is live?
- what may the agent change?
- which action needs approval?
- what evidence proves the result?

XPeX is building the composition and governance layer that turns fragmented software into specialized digital workers.

## What are we building?

The platform combines:

- **XPeX Systems Command** — Company Intelligence & Evidence OS;
- **GX** — AI orchestration and system-intelligence interface;
- **XPeX Plugin Factory** — compiler for plugins, MCP configurations and skills;
- **GX Neural Copilot** — the live plugin/MCP bridge between ChatGPT/Codex and XPeX company intelligence;
- **Evidence First** governance — runtime, source, security and approval boundaries;
- a future **Capability Graph** mapping tools, permissions, risk and evidence requirements.

The long-term product direction is a **Neural Workforce**: reusable digital workers assembled from agents + skills + plugins + external apps under one policy.

## Why now?

AI models are becoming capable enough to operate software.

MCP and plugin ecosystems make tools increasingly composable.

The bottleneck is moving from “can the AI call a tool?” to:

> **Can an organization safely compose tools into work and prove what happened?**

XPeX is being built for that transition.
