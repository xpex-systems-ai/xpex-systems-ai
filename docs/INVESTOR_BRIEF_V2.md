**Company:** XPeX Systems AI · **Founder:** Junior Sena

## Thesis

Companies need AI capabilities that can connect to existing systems with explicit identity, permissions, runtime evidence and human approval. XPeX assembles Company Intelligence, agent/MCP integration, technical audits, creative workflows and machine-service distribution into one governed architecture.

GXEON is the agent economy layer inside the company. It observes demand and public integration states, organizes technical capabilities and preserves the distinction between an opportunity, an accepted delivery and a settled receipt.
