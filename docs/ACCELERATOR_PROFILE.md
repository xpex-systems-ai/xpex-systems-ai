# XPeX Systems AI — Accelerator Profile

## Company

**XPeX Systems AI**

**Category:** AI Systems, Agents & Company Intelligence  
**Tagline:** Build. Connect. Operate. Prove.

## Elevator pitch

XPeX Systems AI is building an evidence-aware operating layer for AI-native companies: a control plane that maps systems and infrastructure, governs autonomous agents, routes intelligence across providers and preserves verifiable evidence of what was deployed, executed and approved.

## Problem

AI adoption is creating a new form of enterprise fragmentation.

Companies increasingly rely on:
- multiple model providers;
- coding and operational agents;
- cloud/deployment platforms;
- databases and vector stores;
- plugins/MCP/connectors;
- autonomous workflows.

The intelligence is improving faster than the governance and operational control around it.

## Solution

XPeX combines:

- **Systems Command** — company intelligence and evidence;
- **GXEON** — governed execution by agents;
- **NEXARA** — target model/provider routing layer;
- **XAGF** — governance and security framework;
- **vertical products** — education, revenue, Web3 and media.

## Initial wedge

The near-term wedge is **AI Systems / Digital Asset Audit**:

> Find, classify, correlate and prove the systems a company owns and operates.

This creates immediate service revenue potential while feeding the software control plane.
