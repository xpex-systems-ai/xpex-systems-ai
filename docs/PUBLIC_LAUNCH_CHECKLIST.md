## Launch rule

Do not launch the company by showing everything.

Launch it by showing a coherent chain:

```text
WHO WE ARE
  → WHAT WE BUILD
  → 3 DEEP DEMOS
  → 5–7 FLAGSHIPS
  → SECURITY / TRUST
  → FOUNDER
  → CONTACT / NEXT ACTION
```
