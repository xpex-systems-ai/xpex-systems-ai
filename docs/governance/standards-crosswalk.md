# XAGF Standards Crosswalk

XAGF is an internal XPeX framework. This document shows design alignment only; it does **not** claim certification or legal compliance.

## NIST AI RMF

XAGF maps naturally to the NIST functions:

- **GOVERN** → charter, ownership, policies, decision rights, third-party governance.
- **MAP** → system inventory, intended use, data classes, affected parties, risk tier.
- **MEASURE** → evaluations, red-team, runtime metrics, security testing, evidence.
- **MANAGE** → authorization gates, mitigation, monitoring, incident response, retirement.

NIST's Generative AI Profile further informs generative-AI risk treatment and evaluation.

## ISO/IEC 42001

XAGF adopts management-system concepts such as:
- governance and accountability;
- AI risk assessment and treatment;
- lifecycle controls;
- documented objectives and responsibilities;
- monitoring and continual improvement;
- supplier/third-party considerations.

XPeX does not claim ISO/IEC 42001 certification unless independently certified.

## OWASP Generative / Agentic AI Security

XAGF addresses agentic attack surfaces including:
- reasoning and instruction manipulation;
- memory;
- tools;
- identity and permissions;
- human oversight;
- multi-agent interaction;
- excessive agency;
- data exfiltration and prompt injection.

## EU AI Act

XAGF incorporates risk-based ideas relevant to:
- risk management;
- technical documentation;
- logging/traceability;
- human oversight;
- robustness/cybersecurity;
- transparency for direct AI interaction and generated/manipulated content.

Applicability depends on the role, use case, jurisdiction and effective date. Legal compliance requires case-specific assessment.

## Supporting security practice

XAGF is designed to coexist with:
- secure SDLC;
- zero-trust identity;
- privacy management;
- incident response;
- software supply-chain controls;
- vulnerability management;
- business continuity.
