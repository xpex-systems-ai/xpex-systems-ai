# Diagnóstico XPeX de Sistemas e Integrações — Proposed Pilot

Status: PROPOSED. This is a scoping and acceptance template, not a signed contract or a claim of customer demand.

## Buyer and result

Buyer hypothesis: founder or technical manager of a software company with distributed applications, integrations and agents. Expected result: an evidence-backed inventory, prioritized problems and a reviewed correction plan.

## Scope before access

Agree the legal parties, exact systems/accounts, read permissions, allowed data classes, exclusions, retention, delivery date, responsibilities and escalation contact. Obtain scoped customer authorization before connecting to private accounts. Price, taxes, SLA and remedies require an approved proposal and contract.

## Delivery

1. Capture the authorized starting inventory and the customer's current time spent investigating it.
2. Correlate source, runtime, deployment, owner and evidence. Preserve unknown fields.
3. List findings with impact, evidence and a proposed next action.
4. Review conclusions and disagreements with the customer.
5. Deliver the map, findings, correction plan and the measurement record.

Implementation is a separate scope. No permission to change systems follows merely from read access.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every in-scope system has a unique identity and source/runtime references or an explicit unknown state.
- Findings point to evidence with a date and scope.
- The customer reviews the deliverables and records acceptance or remaining issues.
- Actual effort, API/provider costs and the measured benefit are recorded.
- Publication of a customer name, testimonial, screenshot or result requires their explicit authorization.

## Economics

`Estimated delivery cost = hours × hourly cost + APIs/services + allocated infrastructure + fees/other costs`

`Estimated contribution = proposed price − estimated delivery cost`

`Contribution margin = estimated contribution / proposed price`

Use the public calculator for explicit scenario inputs only. It is not net profit, tax advice, company valuation or settled revenue. Unknown amounts remain null/unverified. Record actual costs and payment evidence privately after delivery.

## Commercial evidence lifecycle

PROPOSED → SCOPED → CONTRACTED → DELIVERED → ACCEPTED → INVOICED → SETTLED.

Each state requires its own evidence. A contact, offer listing, approval to work or checkout URL does not establish payment. Confirmed receipts need provider settlement and reconciliation; USDC, RTC and BRL retain their own origins and currencies.
