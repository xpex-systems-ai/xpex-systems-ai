# Fundraising & Partner Readiness

## Objective

Prepare XPeX Systems AI to engage customers, strategic technology providers, accelerators, grant programs and investors without overstating maturity.

## Minimum investor-ready package

### Company
- legal/entity status clearly described
- founder profile
- company thesis
- market category
- product architecture

### Product
- 4–7 flagship systems
- live demos where appropriate
- canonical repositories
- deployment evidence
- product roadmap

### Traction
- customers
- pilots
- signed agreements
- paid revenue
- usage
- retention
- pipeline

Only verified figures are included.

### Security
- secret-management baseline
- access model
- backup strategy
- incident response plan
- software supply-chain policy
- third-party/fork attribution

### Data Room
- manifesto
- architecture
- product map
- cap table when applicable
- financial model when applicable
- contracts
- customer evidence
- technical audits
- IP/ownership register
- roadmap
- fundraising materials

## Partner strategy

Priority categories:
- AI model providers
- cloud/deployment providers
- data infrastructure
- developer ecosystems
- agent ecosystems
- education partners
- channel partners

Relationship status must be recorded accurately.

## Financing paths to evaluate

- bootstrapped revenue
- customer-funded development
- accelerators
- cloud/startup credits
- innovation grants
- strategic partnerships
- angel/pre-seed
- seed funding

Funding is not the first proof of value. Revenue and product evidence remain primary signals.
