# Public Launch Plan V1

## Goal

Launch XPeX Systems AI publicly as a credible AI-native systems company with a small set of verified products and a strong evidence trail.

## Launch gate

Do not launch the full public portfolio until:

- 4–7 flagship systems are selected;
- each has a canonical identity;
- source position is known;
- runtime status is verified;
- security blockers are resolved or disclosed internally;
- public copy is approved;
- public metrics are evidence-backed;
- at least one clear enterprise call-to-action exists.

## Launch sequence

### Stage 1 — Foundation
Private company repo, governance and verified portfolio.

### Stage 2 — Controlled Preview
Private demos to customers, partners and trusted reviewers.

### Stage 3 — Public Company Launch
Website + LinkedIn + X + flagship systems.

### Stage 4 — Enterprise Outreach
Targeted conversations with customers, accelerators, technology programs and strategic partners.

### Stage 5 — Fundraising Readiness
Only after product proof, commercial evidence and a clear use-of-funds thesis.
