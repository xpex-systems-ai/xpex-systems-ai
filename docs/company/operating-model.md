# AI-Native Operating Model

## Founder / Human Operator

Responsible for:
- company strategy;
- financial authorization;
- critical account access;
- legal commitments;
- partner commitments;
- production risk acceptance;
- destructive actions;
- final product promotion;
- final merge approval for critical changes.

## GX / AI Architecture & Audit

Responsible for:
- architecture;
- inventory;
- classification;
- evidence analysis;
- cross-platform correlation;
- technical planning;
- implementation where authorized;
- independent verification;
- portfolio truth.

## GXEON Agents

Responsible for bounded operational work:
- research;
- code tasks;
- monitoring;
- support;
- sales operations;
- content workflows;
- audits;
- integrations.

Agents operate under explicit permissions and policy.

## Approval model

Execution and approval remain separate.

For material changes:

`Propose → Execute → Verify → Human/Independent Approval → Promote`

## Agent maturity

- L0 — Assistant: recommends only
- L1 — Read-only Agent: observes and analyzes
- L2 — Bounded Operator: performs reversible actions
- L3 — Workflow Agent: executes approved multi-step flows
- L4 — Autonomous Domain Agent: acts continuously under policy and budget
- L5 — Strategic Autonomy: not enabled by default; requires explicit governance

## Cost discipline

Every autonomous workflow should eventually report:
- cost;
- model/provider;
- tool usage;
- duration;
- output;
- business result;
- confidence;
- evidence.

Autonomy without economics is not an enterprise system.
