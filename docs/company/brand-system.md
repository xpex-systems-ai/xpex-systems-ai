# XPeX Systems AI — Brand System V1

## Primary brand

**XPeX Systems AI**

Do not replace the company name with Nexara.

## Master tagline

**Build. Connect. Operate. Prove.**

## Descriptor

**AI Systems, Agents & Company Intelligence**

Alternative enterprise descriptor:
**Autonomous Intelligence Infrastructure**

## Product naming

- XPeX Systems Command
- GXEON
- NEXARA Intelligence Fabric
- XPeX Academy
- XPeX Revenue
- XPeX Web3
- XPeX Media / Creator

## Visual system

Primary:
- near-black background
- warm metallic gold
- white typography

Secondary:
- muted graphite
- restrained green for verified/live
- restrained amber for review
- restrained red for blockers

## Design rule

Gold signals identity and hierarchy.
Green signals verified state.
Neither color substitutes for evidence.

## Voice

Confident.
Technical.
Minimal.
Global.
Evidence-led.

Avoid:
- fake urgency;
- unsupported superlatives;
- fabricated partnerships;
- invented numbers;
- crypto-style hype as a default company tone.
