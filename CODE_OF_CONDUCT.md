# XPeX Systems AI — Code of Conduct

XPeX Systems AI is building an engineering and AI-governance community around evidence, technical rigor and responsible execution.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:
- communicate professionally;
- disagree with ideas without attacking people;
- distinguish evidence from assumptions;
- disclose material conflicts of interest;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- avoid publishing secrets or exploitable security details;
- preserve attribution for third-party and open-source work;
- report safety/security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment or discriminatory abuse;
- deliberate deception about evidence, ownership, security, customers, revenue or partnerships;
- credential exposure;
- malicious exploitation of project infrastructure;
- knowingly bypassing governance controls without authorization;
- claiming another party's work as proprietary XPeX intellectual property.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may restrict or remove participation that materially violates these rules.

Security incidents should follow `SECURITY.md`, not public discussion when sensitive details are involved.
